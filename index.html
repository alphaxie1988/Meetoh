1 How CCA Selection Works at Mee Toh

Summarised from the school's official FAQ letter (29 July 2026) and CCA Awareness/Selection Day letter (14 July 2026).

1

Attend CCA Awareness Day

4 Aug 2026, 2.15–3.45pm. Booths for every CCA are set up at the Indoor Sports Hall (L1) and Multi-Purpose Hall (L2). Up to 2 parents may accompany each child.

2

Choose 6 CCAs, 2 per Group

Pick exactly 2 CCAs from each of Group 1, 2 and 3 — 6 choices total — ranked in order of preference. Submitted via the All Ears platform from 4 Aug 2026.

3

Trial only if 1st choice is oversubscribed

If your 1st choice CCA is oversubscribed (more applicants than vacancies), your child attends a selection trial on 17 Sep 2026. Other oversubscribed choices (2nd–6th) are decided by balloting, not trials.

4

One CCA, until end of P6

Each child is allocated only one CCA, informed via Parent Gateway in Term 4. Once assigned, students stay in that CCA until the end of Primary 6 — so choose thoughtfully!

⚠️ Worked example from the school's own FAQ

A student ranks Choir (1st), Library (2nd), Chinese Dance (3rd) — and both Choir and Library happen to be oversubscribed that year:

  • Choir is oversubscribed → child attends a trial. Gets in only if they pass.
  • If they don't get Choir, they are not given Library either — its vacancies (filled via trial for students who put Library 1st) are already gone.
  • They instead drop to their 3rd choice, Chinese Dance — and if that's also oversubscribed at that point, it's decided by balloting.

Takeaway: only your true first choice gets a fair shot via trial. Choices 2–6 for popular CCAs are essentially a lottery — so rank realistically, not just aspirationally.

4 Tips for Parents

🧭 Making the 6 choices work for you

  • Rank your true 1st choice honestly. Only your 1st choice gets a fair trial; later choices for popular CCAs are decided by balloting, not merit — so don't "waste" your 1st choice on a CCA your child isn't genuinely excited about just because it seems easier.
  • Mix a "reach" choice with safer choices. If your child's heart is set on a historically oversubscribed CCA (Badminton, Basketball, Robotics, Wushu, Modern Dance, Art & Craft Club, Odyssey Cub Scouts), put it 1st — but make choices 2–6 activities that are rarely oversubscribed, so your child isn't left purely to chance.
  • Remember: one CCA, six years. Since the allocation lasts until the end of P6, weigh long-term fit (interest, temperament, physical demands) over short-term excitement.
  • Check the target-group rules. A few CCAs (e.g. Robotics is generally for P5/P6, Floorball/Basketball/Badminton have age-banded session days) affect which day your child trains and who their teammates are.
  • Factor in logistics. Sessions run 2.15–4.15pm on Tue/Thu (some Wed); school-team members may train 3x a week. Plan lunch, transport and any tuition clashes in advance.
  • Talk to the booths on Awareness Day. This guide is a starting point — nothing beats a first-hand chat with the teachers-in-charge and current student members on 4 Aug.
